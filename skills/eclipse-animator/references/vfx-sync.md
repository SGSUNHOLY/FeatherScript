# Animation and VFX synchronization

Plan anticipation, contact and recovery in seconds before keying. Put named markers such as Windup, Hit and Recover on the authored timeline; bind client effects to GetMarkerReachedSignal, and disconnect listeners when tracks stop or characters respawn. Damage remains server validated. A marker is presentation timing, not proof of a hit.

Preserve the original sequence; create a named revision. Inspect first/contact/last poses and root motion. Check loop seams for idles and foot sliding for locomotion. Use impact frames only on confirmed contact, with a reduced-flash option. Review both the animation alone and the combined effect at normal speed.
